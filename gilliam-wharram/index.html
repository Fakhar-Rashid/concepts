Levels of service

Compilation, review or audit?

Three levels of service for your financial statements, from statements presented with no assurance to our highest level of assurance.

Compilation
Review
Audit

Level of assurance

No assurance

Compiled financial statements

Information that is the representation of management or owners of a business, presented in the form of financial statements. No procedures are undertaken to express any assurances on these financial statements.

Limited assurance

Financial statement reviews

A practical alternative for the client who does not require a complete audit, but does require complete financial statements with full disclosure, prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles. Reviews include inquiries of management and analytical procedures.

Highest level of assurance

Financial statement audits

Procedures performed in accordance with generally accepted auditing standards or governmental auditing standards, to express an opinion as to whether the financial statements are in conformity with professional standards in all material respects.

Special examinations. Agreed-upon procedure engagements, bank directors' examinations, internal audits and more.
