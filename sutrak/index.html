Electric HVAC for bus

What's all the hype about?

Operational and efficiency benefits are at the heart of the matter. SUTRAK has the longest running experience in production deliveries of all electric HVAC systems.

Nearly half

of the average bus's life is spent at idle. Electric HVAC can deliver full capacity while idling, when it is needed most.A bus at a stop for one minute on a very hot day, doors open, with heat soaked passengers getting on board.

  1. #1

    Full capacity at idle

    Conventional compressor output follows engine RPM, so conventional systems are heavily restricted at idle. Electric systems maximize performance while idling.

  2. #2

    Self-contained designs

    Locating the compressor inside the HVAC package eliminates the refrigerant tubing and its potential leak points. It speeds up installation and improves testing and reliability.

  3. #3

    Scroll compressors

    Electric scroll compressors handle liquid better than piston or screw designs, with fewer moving parts. Their hermetic design eliminates shaft seal problems.

With the advent of battery electric buses, SUTRAK is well positioned to provide electric HVAC designs, backed by nearly two decades of electric HVAC design, manufacturing and operation in the field.

SUTRAK's story

A run of firsts

Nearly 50 years of global design, innovation and uncompromising performance, and a leading manufacturer of HVAC systems for buses, light rail vehicles and automated people movers.

  1. 1970s

    Roof mount, patented

    SUTRAK patented roof mounted HVAC technology for buses. Before this, only skirt mounted and rear mounted systems existed. SUTRAK expanded to 6 continents, and roof mounted technology is now ubiquitous around the globe.

  2. 1981

    Manufacturing in Colorado

    SUTRAK established a manufacturing site in Colorado. It served as the North American headquarters until the 2006 expansion.

  3. 1996

    Independently owned

    After a corporate sale of the international divisions, SUTRAK Corporation (SUTRAK USA) became independently owned.

  4. 2002

    Electric, presented in Las Vegas

    SUTRAK presented its electric technologies to the U.S. transit industry, which led to a series of firsts for production buses in the United States:

    • The first production self-contained rear mount HVAC on gas electric buses (New Flyer ISE)
    • The first self-contained HVAC to integrate dual scroll compressors for production buses

  5. 2006

    Generator-powered, articulated

    The first generator-powered electric HVAC on articulated diesel buses (New Flyer).

  6. 2007–2008

    Generator-powered, 40-foot

    The first generator-powered electric HVAC on 40-foot diesel buses (NABI), with multiple projects from 2009 to 2019.
