Our family

A Greenville family business since 1955

  1. 1955

    Sam Duncan began the company along with his wife Margaret, and served Greenville and the surrounding area until his death in 1997.

  2. 1977

    Their son Sammy Duncan joined the business after his graduation.

  3. 1990

    Sammy's wife Sandra began working in the business full time.

  4. Today

    Sammy and Sandra operate the family-owned business. They have trained their three sons as electricians, prepared to carry on the business if that is their future desire.
