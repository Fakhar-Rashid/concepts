Business opportunities

Buy or sell an existing business, with or without real estate.

We can help you all the way through the process.

Selling For sellers

  1. Gather the information a buyer typically needs
  2. Put financial information in an appropriate and marketable format
  3. Help decide on a realistic price
  4. Suggest ways to improve the marketability of the business
  5. List the business in the appropriate business and real estate databases
  6. Contact our list of past potential business buyers
  7. Help negotiate price and other conditions of the sale

Buying For buyers

  1. Help find the right business for you
  2. Help find financing if needed
  3. Research the appropriate price for a specific business
  4. Write up a Letter of Intent (LOI) to help negotiate the terms of purchase

The broker cannot legally draw up a contract for the sale of a business, so the buyer and seller or their attorneys will need to draw up the final contract.
