How tax time works

The CPAs are available by email or phone to answer questions while you gather your tax documents.

  1. Ask for your organizer

    Request this year's tax organizer. Email is our preferred way to send it, and mail or fax work too.

  2. Gather your documents

    Wait until you've received all of your tax documents for the year. The checklists below show what to bring together.

  3. Send them all at once

    Upload everything in one go, or drop it off at the office during office hours. After hours, slide it under the door. Envelopes are provided if you didn't bring one.

  4. Your tax partner reviews

    Your tax partner will reach out with any questions or concerns while reviewing your documents.

  5. Go over it together

    Call 303-841-4220 to set up an appointment to go over your tax documents together on the phone.
