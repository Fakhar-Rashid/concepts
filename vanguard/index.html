More on fire alarm

Cellular monitoring

Current life safety codes allow cellular as the sole path to the central station. It is less exposed to weather, can't be cut off by falling trees, and can save hundreds of dollars a year.

Wireless devices

For areas where wiring is difficult or undesirable, wireless devices avoid unsightly runs and still meet life safety requirements.

Service

Testing and inspection, smoke detector cleaning and sensitivity testing, critical parts stocking, repair, replacement and emergency service.

More on clean agent

Why not water

A sprinkler can save the building, but the water damage stays. The bigger cost is downtime, lost client information, and equipment and software you can't replace.

Designed per room

Each Siemens FM-200 or Novec 1230 system is designed around health and environmental concerns, space limitations and serviceability.

Touch-screen keypads, door and window contacts, motion and glass-break detectors, and cellular alarm communication. User codes show who armed and disarmed the system.

How a new project runs

We join developers, architects and consultants early, so the life-safety design is settled before construction starts.

  1. Pre-planning

    We assess the building, select components and produce the specifications and drawings.

  2. Installation

    A project manager, installers, electricians and technicians are assigned to hit your schedule.

  3. Training

    On-site training for the people who will run the system every day.

  4. Service

    Inspections, maintenance and 24/7 support for the life of the system.
